# Brief v1 — Feedback from Feli's Molloy onboarding call

**Date captured:** June 3, 2026
**Source:** Feli (Felicitas Luna), Member's Strategist on the Molloy Roofing account
**Brief tested:** [`molloy-roofing-onboarding-brief.html`](./molloy-roofing-onboarding-brief.html) (v1)
**Call status:** Rescheduled — call ran later (with Feli using parts of the v1 brief). Next touchpoint scheduled for July.

---

## What worked

- **The questions to ask.** Feli actively used several. Made everyone talk on the call (3 people on the line).
- **The "What NOT to do" section** — kept her from over-pitching.

## What didn't work / was over-engineered

- **Too much info overall.** The brief was too long. She didn't read it cover-to-cover, scanned only.
- **The hypothetical 4-hire roadmap was too much.** She didn't use it. Felt too prescriptive / forecasted. Made her worry the call would feel rehearsed.
- **Industry context + long quote lists** weren't referenced live.
- **The brief tried to do too many jobs at once.** Pre-call primer + cheat sheet + strategy doc all in one = none of them done cleanly.

## What she wants in v2

> "Explain it like I'm five."

- **Who is who** — plain language, one line per person
- **What shouldn't be repeated** — don't re-ask things sales already covered
- **Simple language**, not corporate
- **Where the focus should be** — one focus, not a kitchen sink
- **What questions to ask** — keep this prominent

## What she learned on the call (worth capturing for the next touchpoint)

- 3 people on the call (Brian Holtman confirmed as attendee)
- A lot of long-tenured staff at Molloy — she heard crucial names mentioned. *(Names should be captured into the Members record after every touchpoint — TBD where, but probably a "Key people" field on the Member record that grows over time.)*

## Implications for the spec

This feedback drives a revision to [Tool 16 · Member Intelligence Enricher](../ai-tools/16-member-intelligence-enricher.md). The output format compresses significantly. See updated spec for the v2 Brief template.

Key principle: **the brief is a primer, not a script.** It should fit on one page, take under 3 minutes to read, and leave the AP feeling prepared without feeling rehearsed.
