AP Deep-Dive · 1 of 3
The v2 onboarding call — relationship first
The first call between an AP and a new member is the moment the partnership is built or lost. v1 was vendor-flavored (hiring intake at minute 3). v2 is a real partnership intake (first hiring question at minute 30+). The relationship that comes out of this 60–75 min call is worth a 12-month retention curve, not a one-time placement.
v1 (vendor-flavored)v2 (real partner)
Bond → role discovery → budget → processBond → their story → the business → the team → role → roadmap
First hiring question at minute 3First hiring question at minute 30–35
AP gets the brief, leavesAP knows the founder's "why," not just the JD
Member feels: efficientMember feels: heard
The 6 blocks:
Block 1 · Real Opening
5–8 min
Specific personal reference from the brief (the kid, the marathon, the press hit). AP shares something equally personal back. Permission-set: "I want to spend the first half on your business before we get to hiring." Don't move on until the founder has laughed or shared something they didn't have to share.
Block 2 · Their Story
12–18 min
Understand the founder. Not the company yet. The person. Anchor: "Walk me through how this started." Listen 3x more than you talk. Notes on paper, where the founder can see you taking them.
Block 3 · The Business Itself
10–15 min
Understand the company through the founder's lens. Customers, unfair advantage, where it's growing, where the friction is. Bridge question: "Where do you want this company in 3 years?"
Block 4 · The Team Today
8–10 min
No hiring talk yet. The org as it exists. Reports-to, longest-tenured, where it's stretched. The founder organically tells you their hiring problem here.
Block 5 · Personality, Roadmap, Growth Lever
8–10 min
Where the AP earns the consultant title. Key question: "You're hiring this seat now. Tell me what the next seat is. And the one after." If the founder doesn't have a roadmap, the AP offers a hypothesis. That's the renewal anchor.
Block 6 · Co-Design + Close
5–7 min
Promise the 24-hour commitment doc. Confirm first drop. Schedule 30-day strategic check-in on the call. Final close — warm and personal, not corporate.
Block · Role mechanics
Handled by TA
Day-to-day, tech stack, reports-to, "great at 90 days" — handled by the TA in a separate role-intake call. Not in the onboarding.
Block · Budget + process
Handled by sales/DP
Rate confirmation, drop cadence, payment terms — handled before close by sales, or in a short follow-up. Keeps the onboarding call pure: relationship + strategy.
AP Deep-Dive · 2 of 3
The Member Brief — what Tool 16 produces 24h before the call
Tool 16 (Member Intelligence Enricher) runs 24 hours before the onboarding call and lands a deep brief in the AP's inbox. The brief is built for the v2 call — relationship-first, not hiring-first. It loads up on personal hooks, founder story, and business context so the AP walks in feeling like Sagan has already invested in the relationship.
For Block 1 · Real Opening
Personal hooks pulled from sales transcripts + LinkedIn + recent press. Pick one. Be specific. "Sarah ran the NYC marathon in November" / "Tom is a Cowboys fan, saw it on his Zoom background."
For Block 2 · Founder Story
Where they grew up, prior companies, why they started this one. Public clues from LinkedIn posts about how they think. The "why" before the AP asks.
For Block 3 · The Business
Stage, funding, growth rate, customers, the unfair advantage in their own words from sales transcripts. Industry context (what's happening in their market).
For Block 4 · The Team
LinkedIn org map. Recent hires + leadership churn. Educated guess on the pain point the founder will surface about who's drowning vs. who's solid.
For Block 5 · Roadmap Hypothesis
Based on similar Sagan members at the same stage: what does hire #1, #2, #3 look like? What AI agent builds make sense? Ready to surface if the founder doesn't have one.
What the AP brings personally
3-item checklist for the AP: one personal opener, one thing to share back about themselves, one roadmap hypothesis ready for Block 5. Walks in with conviction.
What the brief tells the AP NOT to do: don't lead with the hire, don't promise transactional numbers early, don't reference sales transcripts directly ("you said X") — use the signal, don't show your homework.
