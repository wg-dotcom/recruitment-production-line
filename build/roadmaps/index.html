Three formats to choose from

If the recommended layout above doesn't feel right, here are two alternatives.

Option A · Two-track quarterly grid

(What you see above.) Two horizontal tracks (Hires + AI Agents Builds), four quarter columns. Each cell holds card items colored by confidence.

Why this: shows both roadmaps at once, scannable in 5 seconds, quarter granularity matches how members actually think about hiring, easy to render + update inside the Member Portal.

Trade-off: less precise than month-level timing.

Option B · Gantt-style timeline

Horizontal bars by month, each bar representing one role or AI build. Length = duration of search/build. Color = confidence.

Why this: precise timing, visually rich, classic project-mgmt format.

Heavier to render (needs a Gantt lib), needs precise dates that members rarely commit to. Probably overkill for v1.

Option C · Mermaid timeline (text-based)

Mermaid diagram in the Member Portal doc. Live-renders from markdown. Editable by anyone who can type.

Why this: zero custom code, embeds anywhere markdown renders (Notion, GitHub, the portal).

Looks more dev-tool than product, harder to make beautiful.
