Recruitment
as a Production Line

Sales & Operations Planning applied to recruitment. Four parallel workstreams. One execution gate. A 30-year-proven model.

Prepared by Jesus Pacheco For Leadership Team
Where we are today

TAs are doing five jobs at once.

Sourcing, vetting, polishing, presenting, and managing the member relationship — all at the same time. Works at low volume. Breaks when we scale.

  • Quality drifts because no one owns one thing well.
  • Delivery times are unpredictable because there's no flow.
  • Bottlenecks are invisible because everything is tangled together.
The Framework

We're not inventing this.
We're applying it.

Sales & Operations Planning. The discipline serious companies use to match what customers will want with what the business can produce.

  • Plan demand. Member roadmaps + Decision Partner signals.
  • Plan supply. Sourcing capacity + Candidate Ops throughput.
  • Match the two. AI matching + stage capacity.
  • Execute. Four parallel workstreams.
  • Review performance. Stage-level metrics like time-to-first-presentation.
Business Case · It Worked Before

Dell's Build-to-Order revolution.

In the 1990s, Dell stopped keeping finished PCs in inventory. Instead, they kept a safety stock of components and assembled on order.

Same logic, different industry. We're applying it to talent.

The Model

Four stages. One flow. A feedback loop.

Stage 01
Sourcing
Active pipelines + Assault Team + Strategic
Stage 02
Candidate Ops
Polished profiles, videos, availability. The safety stock.
Stage 03
Recruitment / TAs
AI matching, presentations, interview prep + reports
Stage 04
Decision Partners
Member strategy, advisory, the close

↻ Stage 4's "next hire identified" feeds back into Stage 1. The line keeps feeding itself.

Why It Wins

Speed. Visibility. Scale.

  • Speed without quality tradeoff. Safety stock of pre-vetted candidates means we present in minutes.
  • Bottlenecks become visible. Slow presentations? Candidate Ops. Slow matching? Recruitment. Fix the stage.
  • Right people, right seats. Recruiters recruit. Decision Partners advise and close. Sourcers source.
  • AI-ready. Each stage compounds with technology. The structure was built for it.
  • Better candidate experience. A team dedicated to polishing profiles. The cream rises.
The Infrastructure

The ready-to-present database.

The safety stock that makes the whole line fast. If this isn't well built, nothing downstream is reliable.

  • Single source of truth. API-accessible. Queryable by matching tool, presentation builder, dashboards.
  • Recommendation: Airtable as v1. Already in use. MCP integration ready. Portable data model.
  • Candidate Ops owns it. Recruitment and Decision Partners read only.
  • Auto-decay. Records refresh weekly. Stale records drop out. The DB stays current.
The Plan

Build in parallel.
One execution gate.

All four workstreams advance at the same time. We run the new model the day Candidate Ops starts delivering.

  • Sourcing — Mostly in place. Pipelines already running. Just needs structure.
  • Candidate Ops — To build. New team. Async-first. Build the AI tooling.
  • Recruitment / TAs — Already performing. Add Pod Leads + matching, presentation, report tools.
  • Decision Partners — To define. ← Primary focus. Bench: Manuel, Manuela, Feli, Miguel.
Risks & Mitigations

Where this could go sideways.

01
The handoff tax
Mitigation: Documented input/output specs. SLAs. Single source of truth.
02
Losing surfacing speed
Mitigation: Continuous feed into safety stock. Assault Team fast-tracks urgent.
03
Can't depend on one person
Mitigation: Stage leads own throughput. We manage the system, not the requests.
